## Usage Instructions

### For Image Collection:
1. **Direct URLs Available**: Apple (10 images), Dell (1 image), ASUS (5 images) = 16 total ready-to-use URLs
2. **Manual Extraction Needed**: Visit product pages for HP, Lenovo, Acer, MSI models and additional Dell/ASUS models
3. **Image Quality**: All available images are high-resolution (800x800 minimum, many exceed 2000px)

### Tips for Manual Extraction:
1. Visit the product page URL provided in the JSON/table
2. Open browser Developer Tools (F12)
3. Go to Network tab and filter for images
4. Right-click on product images and "Open image in new tab"
5. Copy the direct image URL from the address bar
6. Look for images in formats: .jpg, .png, .webp
7. Prefer URLs with parameters like "large", "high", or specific dimensions

All images are property of their respective manufacturers. Use only for authorized machine learning training purposes with proper attribution.
